## 📌 Project Overview

Customer behavior analysis helps businesses understand purchasing patterns, customer preferences, product performance, and shopping trends.

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior using data analytics tools such as Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI.

The goal is to transform raw shopping data into meaningful business insights that can help businesses understand their customers, evaluate purchasing trends, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and shopping patterns.
- Identify popular product categories.
- Explore customer spending patterns.
- Compare purchasing behavior across customer segments.
- Analyze the relationship between discounts and purchasing behavior, if the relevant data is available.
- Create visualizations to communicate business insights.
- Provide data-driven recommendations for improving business performance.
